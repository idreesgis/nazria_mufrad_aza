How to Record Your Pulse Video - Finger Method

1

Prepare Your Phone

Clean your phone's camera lens. Ensure you're in a well-lit room but avoid direct sunlight on the camera.

2

Position Your Finger

Gently place your index or middle finger flat on the camera lens. The finger should completely cover the lens and flash (if available).

3

Apply Light Pressure

Press gently - not too hard, not too light. You should see a reddish glow when the flashlight is on.

4

Record for 30-60 Seconds

Start recording and keep your finger steady. Avoid moving or talking during recording. Longer recordings provide better accuracy.

5

Stay Relaxed

Sit comfortably and breathe normally. Avoid recording immediately after exercise or caffeine intake.

Tips for Best Results

  • Use your phone's rear camera with flash enabled
  • Record in a quiet environment while seated
  • Wait 5 minutes after any physical activity
  • Ensure stable hand position throughout
  • Warm your hands if they're cold

How to Record Wrist Pulse - Traditional Nabz Method

نبض کی ویڈیو بنانے کی رہنمائی

1

Setup Phone on Stand

Mount phone on tripod/stand pointing straight down at table. Position 20-30 cm above the surface.

2

Position Your Wrist

Place LEFT wrist palm-up on dark cloth. Position in center of camera frame. Relax your hand completely.

3

Lighting Setup

Use natural daylight from the side or desk lamp at 45° angle. No harsh shadows. Skin should appear natural color.

4

Place Fingers on Radial Artery

Position 3-4 fingers (index, middle, ring, little) on thumb side of wrist, just below wrist crease. Keep fingers parallel and aligned.

5

Record for 60 Seconds

Start recording, then place fingers. Apply medium, consistent pressure. Stay absolutely still. Don't talk or move.

Critical Tips for Nabz Recording

  • Phone on stand/tripod is mandatory - hand-held won't work
  • Rest arm on stable table - eliminate all movement
  • Keep fingers parallel and aligned on artery
  • Medium pressure throughout - not too hard, not too light
  • Record full 60 seconds minimum (90 seconds ideal)
  • Wait 5 minutes after exercise before recording
  • Best time: Early morning before breakfast
